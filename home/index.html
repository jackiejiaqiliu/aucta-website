About Us

Founded in 2011, AUCTA International Education has helped over 500 students achieve their educational goals by providing specialized consulting services in international education. With headquarters in both Austin and Shanghai, AUCTA has extensive resources in international education, including assistance with undergraduate and graduate program applications, as well as background enhancement.

Why Us?

Ivy League-level Consultant Team

Our essay team, led by advisors from Ivy League institutions, features consultants from top-tier universities globally. They are skilled in crafting and refining essays, ensuring a coherent structure and compelling narrative that resonates with your target institutions. Complementing this, our Chinese advisors from esteemed local universities bring valuable insights, blending Chinese cultural perspectives and ideas into the essay framework, thereby enriching the brainstorming process with students.

High Transparency and Active Engagement

We are one of the very few companies in the industry that offers direct, real-time communication between students and our Ivy League consultants for online inquiries and essay revisions. We prioritize deep student involvement in the application process, upholding 100% transparency and authenticity in our consultancy and originality in materials. Our approach ensures efficient learning and personal growth during the application season.

Unparalleled Track Record

Since its establishment in 2011, AUCTA has excelled in providing elite overseas education services for students targeting prestigious universities in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and other countries. We have aided numerous students from China and beyond in gaining admission to world-renowned universities such as Harvard, Princeton, Yale, MIT, Stanford, University of Chicago, Northwestern, Cornell, Columbia, and more. Our success is reflected in thousands of acceptance letters from leading global institutions.

Exclusive Background Enhancement Resources

With resources in both China and the US, AUCTA offers a unique system for background enhancement and career planning. We provide access to domestic and international research projects, volunteer projects, career planning courses, and connections to top firms in finance, consulting, tech, and more. This approach allows for the creation of distinctive, personalized application materials, enhancing student competitiveness during the application process.

Multinational Presence

AUCTA operates from dual headquarters in Austin, Texas, and Shanghai, China. This multinational presence allows us to provide seamless communication, application assistance, and planning services across various time zones. By synergizing the strengths of both the US and China, we utilize unique resources from each country to offer students firsthand information and personalized guidance during the application process.

Personalized Strategies and Comprehensive Planning

AUCTA is dedicated to customizing application strategies that align with the individual profiles and aspirations of each student, leading to well-suited educational opportunities. Our services extend beyond just application assistance, including interview preparation, U.S. visa application guidance, support with arranging overseas accommodation, and post-education career planning. This holistic approach is designed to ensure a seamless and stress-free transition for our students into international education.
