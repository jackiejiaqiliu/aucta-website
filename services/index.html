Undergraduate Services Overview

  • Premier Mentorship and Advisory Services

    Led by top-tier mentors from prestigious universities globally, our team has main consultants, essay consultants, and assistant consultants from Ivy League-level institutions. We offer students a comprehensive consulting experience, complete with personalized guidance and strategic planning. We guarantee bi-monthly interactions through virtual or in-person meetings, along with daily Q&A availability. Our commitment is to provide focused support during every phase of the application process and deliver prompt, efficient, and expert services.

  • Application Form and Process Support

    Inadequate completion of application forms and improper formatting of information are frequent culprits behind application rejections. Without the right presentation, even stellar achievements can fail to stand out. Our personalized support helps students navigate the complexities of application forms, using precise language to showcase their accomplishments. We ensure meticulous attention to detail in both application documentation and related processes, such as correspondence with admissions officers. By managing these critical tasks, we lighten the application workload for students, allowing them to focus on their strengths and potential.

  • Tailored Academic Strategy and Roadmap

    We craft customized academic strategies for each student based on their unique academic trajectories. Our services include creating a structured reading schedule, planning and scheduling standardized tests, advising on course selections, and more. Our goal is to ensure that students actively meet their academic objectives at every stage of their high school journey, thereby strengthening their profile for the competitive application process.

  • Essay Development and Enhancement Services

    The challenge of crafting an authentic, personal, and unique essay is a pivotal aspect of the application process. Good writing skills are also fundamental to our student’s continued success throughout their academic pursuits in higher education. Under the guidance of our Chinese and US essay consultants, students have access to a collaborative process of brainstorming sessions, writing workshops, and essay revisions. We dive into students’ life experiences, passions, and extracurricular endeavors, engaging in a thorough and multi-dimensional exploration with the students themselves. These rich details are then integrated into essays that not only meet but resonate with the distinct criteria of targeted schools and programs.

  • Extracurricular Activities and Background Enhancement

    US universities place a significant emphasis on the holistic development of students. We specialize in devising extracurricular plans tailored to students’ prospective majors. By considering their distinctive needs, traits, and passions, we offer customized strategies and access to exclusive extracurricular resources. Our range of activities is expansive, covering community service, academic competitions, campus club leadership, artistic expression, and sports participation. These extracurricular engagements are designed to not only complement academic achievements but also enhance the student’s personal narrative, crafting a well-rounded and competitive profile for their application.

  • Interview Coaching

    Interviews are a critical component of the college application process, and they present an opportunity for candidates to personally resonate with their prospective institutions. While a less effective interview can break an otherwise strong candidacy, a standout performance can highlight distinctive personal qualities, elevating the applicant’s desirability to the institution. Our coaching service prepares students for these pivotal interactions, with Ivy League mentors offering comprehensive and personalized training. We empower students to handle interviews with universities confidently, helping them to emerge as articulate candidates who leave a strong, positive impression.

  • Strategic School and Major Selections

    Our integrated mentorship approach, which combines the expertise of main consultants, essay consultants, and assistant consultants, facilitates dynamic brainstorming sessions to assist students in identifying majors that align with their interests and strengths. We curate a list of target universities and corresponding majors tailored to each student’s individual profile. Our planning extends beyond compiling application materials to include considerations for the student’s academic trajectory, major selection, and career aspirations. By adopting a balanced strategy that reflects the student’s comprehensive background and incorporates feedback from both students and parents, we ensure a harmonious and strategic selection process.

  • Document Management and Visa Support

    Throughout the application process, our team of consultants expertly manages the submission of documents and score reports. We guide students in reviewing and sending their documents, guaranteeing timely and precise delivery to admissions committees. Furthermore, our support extends beyond the acceptance offer, ensuring students have the assistance they need as they prepare for their educational journey ahead. Our post-acceptance services include assistance with visa applications, interview coordination, and logistical preparations such as medical exams and housing arrangements for a smooth transition to university life.

  • Enhanced Summer Program Consulting

    Participation in summer programs offered by top US universities can significantly enhance a student’s application profile. With a decade of expertise, we have successfully guided 100+ students into notable summer programs at institutions like Yale, the Wharton School, and Stanford, which has often been a precursor to successful Ivy League admissions. Our personalized service caters to each student’s unique academic needs and progress, aiding in the selection of suitable summer programs. We provide comprehensive support, including program selections, brainstorming, essay development, visa applications, enrollment support, and transcript requests upon program completion.
