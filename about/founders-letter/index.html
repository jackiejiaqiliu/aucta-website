Founder’s Letter

Ten years of dedication has been both our privilege and commitment.

Over the past decade, as China’s study abroad sector has grown increasingly complex, students often find themselves navigating a maze of information, with many study abroad agencies treating them as mere commodities. At Aucta International Education, our foundational principle has been consistent in the past decade: to restore true educational values in the study abroad industry. We offer each student tailored development plans that emphasize enhancing their overall skill set and personal growth, regardless of academic performance or background.

As China progresses into a more advanced economic stage, the landscape of traditional industries is transforming. Emerging sectors such as technology are gaining momentum, reshaping the global economic and cultural scene. In this dynamic and competitive environment, education background at top global institutions is increasingly valued, offering significant advantages in a variety of professional fields.

At Aucta International Education, we are renowned for our professional and comprehensive services. Our expert team is organized into three distinct divisions: an education consultation and services team, staffed by experienced Chinese consultants; an application and academic consultation team, featuring overseas consultants from prestigious Ivy League-level universities; and an international student management team based in Austin, USA, delivering a broad spectrum of educational consultation and management services.

Drawing from my own experience living and studying in the United States and Canada for over a decade, I have gained a deep understanding of the unique challenges that international students encounter. The journey toward academic success extends beyond just gaining admission to prestigious institutions; it is a path characterized by personal development and resilience.

Derived from McGill University’s Latin motto, the name “AUCTA” symbolizes our commitment to fostering growth. We aim to bridge the educational gap between overseas and China, bringing cutting-edge educational practices to our students.

Our mission at Aucta International Education is to foster the growth of each student and assist in realizing the global ambitions of every family we serve. We are committed to nurturing the next generation of leaders — individuals who are not only independent and critical thinkers but also socially conscious and responsible. This dedication is at the heart of our unwavering commitment to their success.

—
Lu Hou
Founder
